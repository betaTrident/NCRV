# Refinement notes

## Loop 1 — v1

All six v1 SVGs were audited and rendered on the warm surface color before review.

### A — Midnight Gate

- **Passed:** one color, path-only construction, 15 anchors, clean audit, broad negative interval, stable near-square footprint.
- **Observed:** the equal-height pillars could be read as pause bars or a static letter H before the interval idea appeared.
- **Adjustment:** offset the two outer time boundaries vertically while keeping the central negative path intact. This introduces a cross-date handoff without adding detail.

### B — Cross-Midnight X

- **Passed:** one color, path-only construction, clear letterform, open center, strong small-size weight.
- **Observed:** four edges landed near 57°/123° rather than deliberate whole-number angles. The central diamond was visually wider than necessary.
- **Adjustment:** reconstruct all four arms on exact 60°/120° slopes and tighten the central handoff while preserving the unmistakable lowercase-x reading in the lockup.

### C — Quiet Arc

- **Passed:** one color, path-only construction, 14 anchors, clean audit, strong favicon weight.
- **Observed:** the rounded vertical terminal made the mark feel more like a UI status glyph and increased the loading-indicator risk.
- **Adjustment:** replace it with a smaller square measured terminal. The arc remains the deliberate continuity option and will retain an explicit familiarity-risk warning.

### Shared lockup

- The outlined Manrope wordmark remained immediately readable and used no live text.
- The audit correctly reports that the horizontal lockup is extra-wide; the separate symbol is the small-size asset, and a stacked lockup belongs to the post-approval kit.
- The Manrope outlines produced excessive decimal precision. v2 rounds generated coordinates to two decimals without changing the visible form.

## Loop 2 — v2 checkpoint

The v2 symbols and lockups were audited, rendered, inspected on light, dark, pattern, photo-like, and Voyix-purple backgrounds, viewed at 16/20/24/32/48/64/96/128/256 px, mirrored, rotated 180°, blurred, placed in browser/app/avatar/header contexts, and shelf-tested against six productivity/collaboration references.

### Audit results

- All three symbol SVGs scored 100/100 in the structural audit with no reported issues.
- The outlined horizontal lockups scored 90/100. Their warnings are limited to two natural Manrope curve edges near 75°/105°, high anchor counts from outlined type, and the expected extra-wide ratio. No live text, rasters, filters, masks, gradients, or excess colors are present.
- B's symbol no longer reports near-miss angles; all four arms now use exact 60°/120° construction.

### A — Midnight Gate

- **16 px:** the two offset boundaries and central interval remain visible.
- **One-color/reversed:** passes; the negative window stays open on black, white, and Voyix-purple fields.
- **Context/shelf:** has the strongest distinction from the blue, circular, contained productivity reference set.
- **Risk:** without the rationale, some viewers may initially see pause bars, opposing brackets, or a doorway. The vertical offset and cross-boundary cut reduce but do not eliminate that risk.

### B — Cross-Midnight X

- **16 px:** retains an immediate `x` reading and open center.
- **One-color/reversed:** passes with no closing gaps.
- **Context/shelf:** compact and highly usable as an icon; the custom wordmark substitution remains legible.
- **Risk:** an `x` is semantically neutral and may be mistaken for close/cancel, multiplication, or a generic initial unless repeated consistently with the full name.

### C — Quiet Arc

- **16 px:** the arc and square terminal remain separate and legible.
- **One-color/reversed:** passes; the broad arc is stable on every tested background.
- **Context/shelf:** strongest continuity with the documented crescent direction.
- **Risk:** reads first as `C·`, an open ring, or a loading/status glyph. Its night meaning is learned rather than inherent, and open-ring territory is already crowded. This is the weakest originality result.

## Recommendation

Advance **A — Midnight Gate**. It best expresses a defined night interval and visible cross-boundary record without literal moon, clock, payroll, or HR imagery. It is also the most distinctive on the shelf and has the clearest path to a broader identity system.

B is the strongest alternative if a name-led, typographic identity is preferred. C should advance only if continuity with the documented crescent territory matters more than category distinctiveness.

These checks do not provide trademark clearance. A professional trademark search and reverse-image search remain required before launch.
